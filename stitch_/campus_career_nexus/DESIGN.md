## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a bridge between academic accomplishment and corporate readiness. Tailored for college students, university career centers, and enterprise recruiters, it combines institutional credibility with forward-looking dynamism.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Corporate Modernism** with an **Energetic Professional** edge:
- **Tone & Demeanor**: Authoritative yet empowering, structured without stiffness, and encouraging rather than intimidating.
- **Visual Tenor**: Clean surfaces, architectural whitespace, razor-sharp typography, and purposeful micro-interactions that instill confidence in early-career applicants.
- **Key Tenets**:
  - *Institutional Trust*: Deep corporate blues reassure educational administrators and top-tier recruiters.
  - *Student Agency*: Vivid cyan accents and crisp, punchy feedback loops keep the interface fluid and modern.
  - *Information Velocity*: Scannable job telemetry, application pipelines, and skill matrixes structured through structured visual hierarchy.

## Colors

The color palette reinforces the platform's career-building purpose using a strict semantic hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#1D4ED8`)**: The bedrock tone symbolizing stability, institutional compliance, and professional clarity. Supported by `#2563EB` for active states and `#3B82F6` for interactive accents.
- **Secondary (`#0EA5E9`)**: High-energy cyan-blue representing youth, breakthrough potential, and forward momentum; applied to interactive badges, match scores, and progress metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm amber-gold reserved for urgent deadlines, starred opportunities, verified credentials, and achievement milestones.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A`)**: High-density slate-navy for critical typography, deep contrasting footers, and structural borders.
- **Backgrounds**:
  - Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50) softens visual strain during prolonged job hunts and application workflows.
  - Surfaces: Pure `#FFFFFF` elevates modular cards, search consoles, and navigation menus.
  - Structural Anchor: `#0F172A` to `#1E293B` powers footers, analytical sidebars, and critical enterprise-facing panels.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes functional legibility, structural order, and high information throughput across dense dashboard layouts and narrative career descriptions.

- **Typeface**: `Inter` is universally applied across display titles, data tables, labels, and paragraph copy to maintain cohesive clarity.
- **Hierarchy Rules**:
  - `display-hero` is reserved strictly for landing portals, recruitment season milestones, and annual reports.
  - `headline-lg` and `headline-md` define job categories, company profiles, and application funnel stages.
  - `body-md` serves as the platform's workhorse for resume snippets, job responsibilities, and system logs.
  - `label-sm` leverages uppercase styling with increased tracking (`0.04em`) for status badges, tags, and categorical pill indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8pt base spacing rhythm** within a responsive 12-column grid system tailored for data density and editorial clarity.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - *Mobile (`< 640px`)*: 4-column layout; gutter `0.75rem`, outer margin `1rem`. Floating application actions stick to the viewport base.
  - *Tablet (`640px – 1024px`)*: 8-column layout; gutter `1rem`, outer margin `1.5rem`. Filter drawers convert to off-canvas sheets.
  - *Desktop (`> 1024px`)*: 12-column layout; gutter `1.5rem`, outer margin `2.5rem` with a maximum content bound of `1280px` (`max-w-7xl`).
- **Layout Patterns**:
  - Split views (e.g., job lists on the left 5 columns, real-time application and job details anchored on the right 7 columns).
  - Dense administrative panels switch to an edge-to-edge full-bleed configuration with sticky sub-headers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **low-contrast boundary definition** and **diffused ambient shadows** tinted with deep navy pigment, avoiding muddy grey drops.

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface)**:
  - Fill: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`
  - Purpose: Secondary cards, quiet list rows, inactive filter boxes.
- **Level 1 (`shadow-sm`)**:
  - Formula: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`
  - Purpose: Primary job cards at rest, input focus containers, persistent top navigation bars.
- **Level 2 (`shadow-md`)**:
  - Formula: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`
  - Purpose: Hover states for actionable cards, dropdown menus, filter popovers.
- **Level 3 (`shadow-lg`)**:
  - Formula: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
  - Purpose: Resume preview modals, floating quick-apply action bars, dynamic notifications.
- **Hover Transitions**:
  - Interactive cards elevate from Level 0/1 to Level 2 using a standard cubic easing curve: `transform: translateY(-2px)`, transition `200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.

## Shapes

The platform adopts a balanced geometry (`roundedness: 2`) that evokes both enterprise precision and welcoming modern software.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to all core controls including text inputs, standard buttons, dropdown toggles, and data grid rows.
- **Container Radius (`1rem` / `16px` - `rounded-lg`)**: Utilized for standard cards, company summaries, and interview timeline units.
- **Prominent Container Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px` - `rounded-xl`)**: Applied to large analytical dashboards, hero recruitment announcement banners, and candidate profile headers.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Reserved strictly for micro-status indicators, career track tags, and applicant stage badges (e.g., "Written Exam", "Offer Extended").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#1D4ED8`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `rounded-md` (`8px`). Hover brings transition to `#2563EB` with `shadow-sm`. Active state settles at `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary**: Crisp outline with 1px `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E293B`. Hover transforms background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#94A3B8`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent surface, text `#2563EB`, hover background `#EFF6FF`.
- **Sizes**:
  - `Small`: Height `32px`, padding `0 12px`, typography `label-md`.
  - `Medium`: Height `40px`, padding `0 16px`, typography `label-lg`.
  - `Large`: Height `48px`, padding `0 24px`, typography `label-lg`.

### Cards & Job Listings
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), and `rounded-lg` geometry.
- Job cards integrate a 3-part vertical split: Company icon & title row, tag cluster (location, salary band, degree requirement), and footer status with application timestamps.
- Card hover triggers a transition to `border-blue-300`, `shadow-md`, and a subtle `translateY(-2px)`.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Standard height `40px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `8px`, typography `body-md`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#2563EB`, accompanied by a diffuse outer focus ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- Input fields host contextual prefix/suffix icons (e.g., search magnifying glass, currency indicators) tinted with `#64748B`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) with concise typography (`label-sm`).
- **Success / Offer**: Surface `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`.
- **Under Review / Processing**: Surface `#EFF6FF`, text `#1D4ED8`.
- **Urgent / High Priority**: Surface `#FEF3C7`, text `#D97706`.
- **Expired / Inactive**: Surface `#F1F5F9`, text `#64748B`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkbox: 16x16px container, radius `4px`. Inactive has `#CBD5E1` border; checked snaps to `#1D4ED8` background with an integrated crisp white checkmark vector.
- Radio: 16x16px circle. Selected displays `#1D4ED8` ring with an inner concentric dot.

### Specialized Career Components
- **Application Stage Stepper**: Horizontal node connector illustrating *Applied -> Resume Screened -> Written Exam -> Interview -> Offer*. Active nodes use `#1D4ED8` with glowing pulse; completed nodes showcase solid green indicators with check marks.
- **Resume Match Gauge**: Compact circular score display leveraging the secondary cyan (`#0EA5E9`) against a neutral muted track (`#E2E8F0`) to instantly communicate applicant-job alignment percentage.
